### Added
- Boss gate dialogue uses a larger framed window. The boss speaks inside a parchment bubble, one word at a time. Click the bubble or press Space to show the whole line. Esc or Enter still starts the fight.
- Boss fights play layered music that opens up as the boss loses life, then fades back to the dungeon when the fight ends.

### Changed
- Item cards spell out Damage, Speed, Defense, Block Chance, Critical Strike Chance, Required Level, and Durability. Upgrades versus what you have equipped are green. Downgrades are red.
- Item names are slightly larger, centered on one line, and drawn without the tan name plate or the side dashes. Long names still wrap.
- A HUD spell button appears only when that slot has a spell. Empty slots stay hidden.
- Barrels, urns, bone piles, and chained skeletons break only when your swing, bow shot, or spell reaches them.
- The big chest after a boss stays closed until you open it. It still pays 100 gold. The coins the boss spills are about half of before, and the chest is the larger share.
- The Hollow Pedlar shows up after every boss, including depth 5.
- The trader's counter is a wide dark panel with gold borders. Stock rows are larger and easier to tap. The sell grid is five columns of satchel slots and scrolls when the pack is longer than twenty items. LEAVE is a red bar at the bottom.
- The satchel's bottom buttons stay put when you select another item. Extra stats scroll inside the card.

### Fixed
- Press-and-hold stats no longer pop up on the Inventory tab. Elsewhere, that card stays where it first opened.
