Controls

Dev menu: F9 or `, or the DEV button. Ignored while a text field is focused, except F9 still toggles.

Play: left-click walk and attack, hold Ctrl to stand still, right-click casts the selected spell. 123 Fireball, Whirlwind, Frost Nova. 6789 Clone, Ward, Shift, Hellmouth once those pages are unlocked. Q/E potions, Space roll, F use, I satchel, M map, Esc pause. -/= zoom.

Touch: drag the left side to move. ATTACK, ROLL, spell buttons, and the DEV button are on screen.

God mode uses the game's own invulnerability flag and holds hunger, thirst, and rest full so starvation cannot bypass it. The hero flickers, the same way Ethereal Shift does. Heal is separate.

Saves use the lichlight_playtest_ prefix. A production cryptdelve character is not read and is not overwritten.
