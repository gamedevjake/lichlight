# Audit: early rare loot and late swing whoosh

Commit `743c82602252546236fa8f468ae1556dbe7b9fcb` (v0.7.18, “Play melee swooshes when the visible swing starts. (#37)”). Read-only. No gameplay, asset, audio, or `version.json` change. `COMBAT_REDESIGN_CHANGELOG.md` was not touched.

Measurement scripts (not part of the game): `tools/audit/wav_onset.py`, `tools/audit/loot_mc.html`, `tools/audit/run_loot_mc.mjs`. Loot figures are 100 seeded floors per depth, generated by the live `startFloor` / `newGame` path in headless Chrome, then cleared by killing trash room by room, opening every chest the key count allows, breaking every pot and cracked wall, opening gilt chests, and killing the Super Elite. “Level held at 1” freezes XP so `lootGated()` stays on. “Clear with XP” grants real `gainXp`. Depth 1 populations matched exactly across those two modes (same seeds).

Rarity index: 0 white, 1 magic, 2 rare, 3 unique. “Rare+” is rarity ≥ 2.

---

## 1. Root causes

### Issue 1 — too many yellows on a new character

A level 1 clear of depth 1 produces **3.38 rare+ items** if XP is held at 1, and **3.53** if the floor’s XP is applied (the character finishes the floor at level 3.06). Median 3, 90th percentile 5, max 6–7. **0 of 100 depth-1 floors had zero rares. 90–91 had two or more.** None of those rares can be worn at level 1. Almost all of them can be worn by the time the floor is finished, because the same floor’s XP reaches the requirement.

The items are not coming from enemy tables, pots, or magic find. Two generators each hand out a yellow on purpose, and one of them fires on the cracked walls the game tells you to break for keys.

#### Root cause A — hidden-passage mouths are paid as greater rares

`placePassages` (depth ≤ 2 wants 1–2 shortcuts, depth ≥ 3 wants 2–3) carves a tunnel and seals both mouths as cracked walls with `passage: true` and `loot: null`:

The tunnel interior is floor, but both ends are walls, so `bfsCount` from the spawn room cannot enter it. `markGatedLoot711` then flags any secret that has a non-wall neighbor outside that flood fill:

```11965:11974:index.html

Every passage mouth has the tunnel tile as that neighbor. On the 100 depth-1 floors, `passage`, `passage && gatedRare`, and `gatedSecrets` were the same number: **2.30**. No non-passage crack was flagged. Depth 2 was the same (2.48 = 2.48 = 2.48). Depth 3 passages were 3.12, all flagged, plus 0.70 other cracks whose neighbor floor sits behind a passage-puzzle slab.

`breakSecret` itself never drops gear (a key, or a 50% torch). The v0.7.11 wrapper does:

So each mouth is a **guaranteed rare**, item level `depth + 2`. Depth 1: item level 3, required level 3 (`floor(0.80 * 2) + 2`). The secret-phase pile in the Monte Carlo was 100% rarity 2, mean 2.30, and it matched the passage count exactly. Breaking both ends of one shortcut pays two yellows. 88 of 100 depth-1 floors paid two or more this way (12 paid zero because no passage survived placement). p50 = 2, p90 = 4, max = 4.

This is the “right after starting” flood. Cracked stone is the key tutorial (`openChest` tells you to look for it). Those cracks are in ordinary rooms, not at the stairs. The v0.7.11 greater-rare was aimed at loot you can only reach by doing a puzzle (see `lockPuzzleBypasses711` and the vault loop in the same function). Passage mouths got swept in because an unopened shortcut looks “unreachable” to a wall-only flood fill.

#### Root cause B — the Super Elite on depths 1–2 is a guaranteed rare, and you must kill it to leave

`seUniqueChance` is 0 at depths 1 and 2, 0.20 at 3, 0.35 at 4, 0.50 at 5, 0.70 through 9, and 1 from 10. A miss, including every depth 1–2 kill, is `makeItem(depth, 2)`:

```

The Monte Carlo hit that exactly: 100/100 depth-1 and depth-2 Super Elites dropped one rare and zero uniques. Depth 3 was 19–21 uniques per 100 and the rest rares (the 20% roll). Item level equals depth. A depth-1 rare requires level 2. `seBlocksDescend717` will not let you take the stairs while it lives, so a full clear always includes this drop. PR #35 (`EARLY_BALANCE_REPORT.md`) changed this from a guaranteed unique to a guaranteed rare. It did not make the drop optional.

#### What does not drop early rares

There is no magic find, luck stat, pity counter, or equipment modifier on any drop roll. Searched the loot path and the affix table: nothing keys off find chance.

`makeItem0` still has an unused fallback (`r < 0.03 + L * 0.003` unique, else `r < 0.15` rare, else `r < 0.50` magic). Every live caller passes a forced rarity, so that curve never runs.

Regular enemies and champions (`regularDrops`, `DROP = { gold: 0, hp: 0.045, mp: 0.04, white: 0.05 }`):

- Trash: one roll. White is the last bucket, 5% before the gate. While `player.lvl <= 2`, only 35% of those whites are kept (`LOOT_GATE.white`), so about 1.75% of non-swarm kills drop a white. Packs halve the rate via `r = Math.random() / swarm`. Never magic, rare, or unique.
- Champions return early. White chance is `0.20 * (gated ? 0.50 : 1)` = 10% while level ≤ 2, 20% after. Plus a 12% key, capped at 1 per floor. Never rare+.
- Mimics are white and do not appear before depth 8.

Pots, barrels, urns, remains (`breakPropsAt`): gold line is a no-op comment, then 5.3% potion, 6% ration, 1% map fragment. No gear. Supply chests are gold and potions only.

Keyed chest, while level ≤ 2 (`chestLoot` + `LOOT_GATE.chestItem` 0.5, `LOOT_GATE.magic` 0.35):

`rareP = min(0.60, 0.15 + depth * 0.012)`. Depth 1 / 2 / 3: **0.162 / 0.174 / 0.186**.

Per keyed chest at level ≤ 2:

| | Depth 1 | Depth 2 | Depth 3 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| No gear (ration or torch) | 0.500 | 0.500 | 0.500 |
| White | 0.325 | 0.325 | 0.325 |
| Magic | 0.147 | 0.145 | 0.142 |
| Rare | **0.028** | **0.030** | **0.033** |
| Unique | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Once level ≥ 3 the gate lifts and every keyed chest is magic or rare: rare = `rareP`, magic = `1 - rareP`, never white, never unique. That is why the XP-on clear’s leftover chests jump from 0.07 rares to 0.21 on depth 1. It is not the main flood. With ~2 keyed chests and scarce keys, expected chest rares stay under a quarter of an item even after the gate lifts.

`ensureSealedLoot` can also call `chestLoot` (60% of an empty sealed region). On depth 1 there are no vaults (`placeVaults` returns at `depth < 2`) and the generation pile had **zero** rares, so this path is not paying yellows on floor 1.

#### Root cause C — from depth 2, every stone vault is rewritten into a greater rare

`markGatedLoot711` runs at the end of `placeV04Post`, after `placeVaults`. For every closed puzzle door that has a rect, if the room does not already hold a rare+, the item is replaced (or created) with `greaterRare711()`:

```11976:11983:index.html
  for (const d of doors) {
    if (!d || !d.puzzle || d.open || !d.rect) continue;
    ...
    if (it) it.item = greaterRare711();
    else ground.push({ ..., item: greaterRare711() });
  }
```

Vaults start at depth 2: `irand(1, 2)` annexes, 62% stone puzzles (`Math.random() < 0.38` are gates and are not rewritten). Measured puzzle rooms: 0.00 / 0.95 / 0.90 at depths 1 / 2 / 3. Generation rares: 0.00 / 0.96 / 0.95. Those yellows are on the ground before the first fight, item level `depth + 2` (depth 2 → item level 4, required level 4). A level 1 character cannot wear them. They are still a yellow beam in the first vault.

Gilt chests (`giltGear`) are a separate, intentional rare: depth < 5 is `makeItem(depth + 1, 2)`, no unique. They spawn on 52–54% of depths 2–3. Opening one did **not** also fire `giveGated711`, because squeeze rooms are placed in `genV072`, after `markGatedLoot711`. One rare per gilt chest, not two.

Islands (depth ≥ 2, 50% attempt, 14–15% placed) use `chestLoot(depth + 1)`, not a forced rare.

#### Required level does restrict equipping. It does not restrict the drop.

`calcReqLvl` (confirmed by calling it inside the same page):

| Item level | White | Magic | Rare |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 1 | 1 | **2** |
| 2 | 1 | 1 | **2** |
| 3 | 1 | 2 | **3** |
| 4 | 1 | 3 | **4** |
| 5 | 2 | 3 | **5** |
| 6 | 3 | 4 | **6** |

Whites of item level ≤ 3 stay required level 1. Magic and rare use `floor(REQ_MULT[r] * (L - 1)) + REQ_ADD[r]` at every level, including 1–3. That is the PR #35 change. Uniques stay on the unique formula and do not drop on depths 1–2.

So the early restriction exists, and it is applied (`makeItem` stamps `reqSrc: 'drop'`). A brand-new level 1 character cannot wear any of the yellows this floor creates:

- Passage greater rare, depth 1: item level 3, requires 3.
- Super Elite rare, depth 1: item level 1, requires 2.
- Vault greater rare, depth 2: item level 4, requires 4.

The XP-on clear ends depth 1 at level 3, and 3.49 of its 3.53 rares were then wearable. The requirement delays the equip until the level-up that the same floor provides. It does not stop the yellows from appearing, and it does not stop a one-floor kit of several rares.

### Issue 2 — the whoosh is heard after the slash streak

PR #37 did move the cue. It is no longer inside the hit. The remaining lateness is the shape of the samples, heard against a streak that is already drawing. The trigger itself is one frame ahead of the first slash pixels.

#### What happens, in order

A tap is not a swing on the press. `held.attack` charges. Releasing under 0.35 s sets `p.tapA` and returns; `armAttack` runs on the next `update` (`updateAttack074`, around the `p.chg >= 0` branch). That is about one frame (~17 ms at 60 fps) of input latency before anything moves or speaks. It delays the picture and the sound together.

`update` then does this in one frame:

1. `p.swing` is reduced by `dt` **before** `updateAttack074` (`update`, the `vis` / hitstop block, then the attack call).
2. `armAttack` sets the swing clock and immediately cues:

```6293:6305:index.html
  const period = attackPeriod();
  p.atkCd = period;
  p.atkPhase = 'windup';
  ...
  p.swingDur = period;
  p.swingHit = p.atkWind;
  p.swingArc = fam.arc > 0 ? fam.arc : 1.1;
  p.swing = period;
  if (p.atkKind !== 'bow') { playSwingCue(); p.swingVoiced = true; }
```

`attackPeriod` is `0.45 / aspd`. Windup time is `period * family.windup`. `playSwingCue` is `sfx('swing', { cue: swingCue() })` with no delay and no buffer offset. `swingCue` is `player_swing_heavy_01` for axe and maul, `player_swing_light_01` for fists, sword, and staff.

3. Because step 1 already ran, the draw in this same frame sees `elapsed = swingDur - swing = 0`. The white streak is the loop `for (a = ang - arc; a < a0; a += 0.12)`. At elapsed 0, `k = 0` and `a0 = ang - arc`, so the loop emits **no pixels**. `drawBlade` still poses the weapon at the back of the arc.

```8649:8656:index.html
  if (p.swing > 0) {
    const dur = ..., arc = ..., hit = ..., elapsed = dur - p.swing;
    const k = elapsed <= hit ? (elapsed / hit) * 0.62 : 0.62 + ((elapsed - hit) / (dur - hit)) * 0.38;
    const a0 = ang - arc + k * arc * 2;
    ctx.fillStyle = 'rgba(255,255,255,.4)';
    for (let a = ang - arc; a < a0; a += .12) ctx.fillRect(...);
    drawBlade(p.x, p.y - 2, a0, 17);
  }
```

4. The next frame (~17 ms later) is the first frame with streak pixels. The buffer has already been scheduled for ~17 ms, plus whatever the audio device adds on top.

5. Damage waits for the windup. `tickAttackSched` counts `atkWind` down and `commitArmed` calls `doAttack`. `doAttack` sees `swingVoiced` and does **not** play a second whoosh. Then it resolves hits and plays `meat` or `whiff`. Impact samples are scheduled `+20 ms` (hit) or `+30 ms` (miss puff, material layer) from that call, not from the swing start.

So the slash streak does appear before the whoosh is loud, but it does **not** appear before the cue call. The cue runs in the arm frame. The streak’s first pixels are the following frame. There is no second cue at the hit.

Heavy peak is the mean of the three (133 ms). “First pixels” is the frame after arm, ~17 ms, when a sword already has a couple of white pixels (`k ≈ 0.13`).

Charged release (`armAttack('power')`) skips the windup. `powerAttack074` calls `doAttack` first (damage and the impact cue), then sets `p.swing = p.swingDur = 0.30`, `p.swingHit = 0.08`, and only then does `playSwingCue` run. Same-frame draw is still elapsed 0, so the streak’s first pixels are still the next frame. The 80 ms mark is `k = 0.62`. A light peak at 56 ms is mid-arc (`k ≈ 0.43`). A heavy peak at 133 ms is **after** that mark (`k ≈ 0.71`), in the recovery, ~50 ms after the charged strike pose. Great Maul’s charged whoosh is the latest of the set. Damage timing itself is unchanged: the hit is resolved before the cue, in the same turn.

Bow is not this bug. `armAttack` does not call `playSwingCue` for `atkKind === 'bow'`.

RMS by slice, light files: about **−35 dB in the first 10 ms**, −25 to −28 from 10–20, −16 to −20 from 20–40, and **−11 to −14 from 40–80 ms**, which is the body. They are quiet again after 160 ms. Heavy files sit around −27 to −30 for the first 10 ms and put the body at **80–160 ms (−12 to −13 dB)**.

There is almost no digital silence to trim. The first sample is over −40 dBFS inside 4 ms on every file. The thing a player calls “the whoosh” is the body, 50–60 ms (light) or 130–140 ms (heavy) after the buffer starts.

#### Playback path

`sfx` logs, then `sfxSample`. A mapped cue returns true even if `smpPlay` cannot start a voice, so a missing buffer is silence, not a late beep and not the procedural swing. `smpPlay` does `BufferSource.start(currentTime + (delay || 0))` with **no offset argument**, so playback begins at sample 0. The swing call passes no `delay`. One-shots also get a random gain of about ±1.5 dB and a playback rate of ±4% (not UI cues). That rate moves a 56 ms peak by about ±2 ms. It does not explain the report.

Variant choice is `smpPick`: random file in the pool, never the same index twice. The four light files and three heavy files share the same shape, so the pool does not create a late outlier.

`player_swing_*` is not in the voice-steal caps. A full 32-voice list drops the oldest voice. It does not queue the new one later.

`AudioContext` output latency is not set anywhere in this file. `start(currentTime)` is “as soon as the device will play.” On a desktop that extra wait is often 20–80 ms and it stacks on the envelope. This VM was not used as a listening test, so that number is not measured here.

Procedural fallback (pack off, `file://`, or `__noSampleSfx`) is an 80 ms triangle starting at `currentTime`, and only if `sfxSample` returns false. With the pack loaded, attacks never reach it.

#### Classification

**Waveform design, heard against a streak that starts one frame after the cue.** Not a late trigger, and not hit detection. Animation length makes it worse for fists and swords: their entire strike picture (54 ms and 81 ms) is over, or halfway over, when the light body peaks. Output latency can add more delay on a real machine. It is not required to explain the report. Trimming leading silence cannot fix it; there is none.

## 2. Recommended fixes and expected effect

### Loot — stop paying passage mouths as greater rares

In `markGatedLoot711`, do not set `gatedRare` on a secret with `passage === true`. That is the whole depth-1 flood outside the Super Elite. Leave the vault rewrite and the unreachable-chest bonus alone if you want puzzle rooms to stay special. Do not change `chestLoot`, `LOOT_GATE`, enemy tables, or the Super Elite in the same change unless the stairs yellow is still too much after this.

Expected rare+ per full clear, level held at 1, passage mouths no longer flagged. Other rows unchanged from the table above.

| | Depth 1 | Depth 2 | Depth 3 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Before | 3.38 | 5.02 | 6.84 |
| After | **1.08** | **2.54** | **3.72** |
| Of which Super Elite | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| Of which cracks | 0 | 0 | ~0.70 (non-passage cracks behind slabs) |

Depth 1 becomes one yellow at the stairs, plus about 0.08 from chests. 12% of floors that already failed to place a passage stay as they are. Floors that used to drop 2–4 crack rares drop none. A clear that spends its XP and opens chests after level 3 goes from 3.53 to about **1.23** (the extra 0.15 is the chest column that already exists).

Depth 2 is still 2.5 rares because the vault rewrite (~0.96), the gilt chest (0.52), and the Super Elite (1.00) remain. Those are behind a puzzle, a squeeze gap, and the stairs, not in the first cracked wall.

### Loot — optional, if floor 2 still feels yellow

Two independent knobs. Either one is enough to try; both together make early yellows actually rare.

1. Vault rewrite only from depth 4. Below that, leave the `chestLoot` the vault already rolled. While level ≤ 2, `chestLoot(depth + 1)` is about 6.5% rare at depth 2. The ~0.96 forced vault rares become about **0.06**. Depth 2 total falls from 2.54 to about **1.64** (Super Elite 1.00, gilt 0.52, scraps 0.12).
2. Super Elite depths 1–2: keep unique chance at 0, but stop the guaranteed rare. A simple curve is 25% rare / 75% magic at depth 1 and 40% rare / 60% magic at depth 2. Depth 3+ stays as it is. Combined with the passage fix only, depth 1 rare+ falls from 1.08 to about **0.33**, and the other 75% of Super Elites drop a blue (required level 1, so it is the thing a new character can actually wear). That is the “occasional” setting. The passage fix alone is the “one exciting rare per floor, at the guardian” setting.

I would ship the passage fix first and leave the Super Elite rare in place. The stairs fight is the moment that was designed to drop a yellow. The cracks were not.

Do not add magic find, and do not put the early gate on `greaterRare711` by level. The bug is which objects are marked, not the player’s level.

### Whoosh — edit the samples, not the swing

`playSwingCue` is already in the right function, at the moment `p.swing` is set, before the first streak pixel. Moving it to “VFX spawn” would make it one frame later. A buffer offset does not help: the first 4 ms are already above −40 dBFS, and skipping ahead 40–100 ms to reach the body would delete the attack and shorten the sound into a snap. Do not change `attackPeriod`, `fam.windup`, reach, or `commitArmed`.

Smallest correction that lines the loud sweep up with the streak:

- Rebuild `player_swing_light_01` .. `04` so the 5 ms RMS is at −20 dBFS by ~10 ms and the peak is by ~25–35 ms. The current body lives at 40–80 ms, which is the entire fist strike.
- Rebuild `player_swing_heavy_01` .. `03` so the peak is by ~40–50 ms instead of 128–142 ms. The charged maul pose is at 80 ms; a 133 ms peak falls in the recovery.
- Keep the four-file and three-file pools and the ±4% rate. No new cue id. `swingCue()` stays as it is.

If assets cannot change in the same pass, there is no code switch that pulls the loud body forward without pitching the files up (`playbackRate` around 1.6–2.0) or delaying the streak so the picture waits for the sample. Delaying the streak would show the hit before the slash. I would not do that.

Expected result if the peaks move to ~30 ms (light) and ~45 ms (heavy): the loud sweep starts as the first streak pixels appear (~17 ms) and peaks while a sword is still early in the windup (`k ≈ 0.23` at 30 ms of an 81 ms windup) and while a maul has barely started (`k ≈ 0.11` at 45 ms of 244 ms). Fists, whose whole strike is 54 ms, get the body at the start of the picture instead of at the hit frame. Damage, reach, and the impact cue stay on the windup expiry.

## 3. Regression tests

Loot, in the existing iframe harness style (`tests/early-balance.html` is the pattern):

- Depth 1, 40 seeds: every `secrets` entry with `passage === true` has no `gatedRare`. Breaking both mouths drops no item of rarity ≥ 2. Keys and the 50% torch still behave as `breakSecret` does today.
- Same seeds: a non-passage crack that is not flagged does not drop gear. A depth-3 secret that is flagged and is not a passage still drops one `greaterRare711` (item level 5, rarity 2), so the puzzle-slab case stays covered.
- Super Elite, seeded `__seUnique`: depth 1 and 2 stay rarity 2, item level = depth, required level 2, one gear item, 25 gold, one ration. Depth 3 stays 20% unique. If the optional 25/40 curve is taken, add a seeded rare-roll the way `__seUnique` already works.
- Level 1 keyed chest: over 200 opens, rare rate within sampling noise of 2.8% at depth 1, unique rate 0, and about half the opens are not gear. Level 3: no whites, rare rate near 16%.
- `calcReqLvl` table above, including item level 3 rare = 3 and item level 3 magic = 2. A white of item level 3 stays 1.
- One stone vault at depth 2: if the rewrite stays, the room contains a rarity 2 of item level 4. If the depth-4 gate is taken, it must not be forced to rarity 2.
